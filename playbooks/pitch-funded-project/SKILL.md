---
name: pitch-funded-project
description: >-
  Use when pitching a freshly funded or shipping project for a paid content,
  community or ambassador role.
---
1. Check <outreach-folder>/tracker.md; skip anyone already contacted.
2. Confirm fit: the project is shipping, recently raised (any size), and has no content person yet. Skip memecoin pump-and-dumps and projects whose content is already covered.
3. Find the right person: founder, CEO or marketing head. Read their bio, pinned post, website and last few posts.
4. Write the DM in the owner's voice (short lines, casual, no hype):
   - line 1: something specific about them (a recent ship, raise, or post)
   - line 2: the gap (story not being told, launch window, no content lead)
   - line 3: proof (follower count, wins, best thread or film link, site)
   - line 4: the ask (paid content/community role or contract)
5. Never offer free work, trials, samples or growth plans.
6. If the X plugin can't start the chat, give the DM to the owner to send manually; if their DMs need verification, write a short public reply under their post instead.
7. After the owner sends, log it in the tracker with date, handle, project and channel, and set a nudge for about 3 days later.
