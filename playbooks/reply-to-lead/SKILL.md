---
name: reply-to-lead
description: >-
  Use when someone the owner pitched replies, sends an offer or brief, or asks
  for samples or rates.
---
1. Read the full reply and any attached brief; check the tracker for the thread's history.
2. Classify: real interest, request for samples, offer with terms, a thumbs-up only, or a likely scam (fake recruiter, asks for payment).
3. Draft one complete ready-to-paste reply in the owner's voice, warm and casual for warm leads:
   - samples: best thread, best film or video, site, key wins
   - offers: compare terms to the owner's rates; counter pay-per-view or commission-only with a flat fee and a small paid test
   - thumbs-up only: wait about 3 days, then a short nudge
   - scams: flag it and suggest asking which company before anything else
4. Never offer free work. Keep it short.
5. Update the tracker with the reply status and the next step.
