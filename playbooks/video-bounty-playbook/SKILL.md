# Video bounty playbook

Patterns from serial video-bounty winners. Use with the Bounty & hackathon breakdown card: fill its 'Best route to winning' line from these rules plus the bounty's own judging criteria.

## Before you start
1. Vet the sponsor: past winners paid, clear criteria, real judging. Don't sink weeks into a contest that looks like free marketing.
2. Read the brief for required length, aspect ratio, tags, language and wording bans.

## Making the video
3. Story before effects: pick the angle and positioning first, then script; show each script line a few visual ways. Narrative beats flashy motion graphics.
4. Length: main entry roughly 2–3.5 minutes; cut 12–35s clips as teasers.
5. Format: 16:9 1080p for sponsor/product explainers; square or cinematic frames for personal stories. Follow the brief if it says vertical.
6. Gear doesn't decide it: a good phone plus CapCut is enough. Use AI tools (grading, cleanup) as helpers, not replacements, and polish hard.
7. Show the real product on screen (screen recordings, real flows).

## Posting
8. Post text: open with a pain-point question or emotional premise, then a call to action and the link. Keep it short.
9. Tag the sponsor and the bounty platform every time.
10. Tease it the same morning ("new video dropping today"), then release; post early if engagement is scored.

## Compounding
11. Build reach daily before you need it: a morning post with a question, reply to everyone.
12. When you win, thank the sponsor by name; turn good sponsor relationships into a recurring series.
13. Use the win record to land paid client work.

Never claim to have watched a video you haven't; mark anything about hooks or editing as unverified unless seen.
